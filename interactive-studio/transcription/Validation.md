Every entered bar accounts for four quarter-note units. All pitch, attack and release values were compared between the exported individual MIDI files and the imported student pack. All 120 measure boundaries matched across the ensemble and every track. Alternate B.C. and T.C. baritone parts matched independently at sounding pitch across all 120 bars. The combined MIDI includes both tracks and silences the duplicate chair by default.

The print review used the original pages, staff-relative transcriptions and an image-based comparison for pitched parts. The private review log records the flagged glyphs. The percussion lines were read separately, including one-bar repeat signs, multi-measure rests, two-voice drum figures and rolls. A repeated-pattern error in Percussion 1 at measures 27–28 was corrected. The last snare grace figure now has the same release ticks in both exports.

“Verified playable” covers pitch or drum assignment, rhythm, transposition, rests, entrances, cutoffs, and measure position. Trumpet 1 measures 116–117 contain a live, half-valve horse whinny. The MIDI carries a cue at that entrance. The other parts continue on time. The two cue measures have no synthesized trumpet pitch.

Playback expression uses interpreted velocity, articulation length, snare-roll subdivisions and dynamics. The slapstick and temple blocks use sampled substitutes. Real-instrument phrasing and exact percussion timbre remain outside this verification.

Browser checks passed for opening the PDF pack, selecting and muting six representative parts, seeking to measures 1, 60 and 120, looping at 150%, opening the 30-page conductor view, section isolation, recording, listening back, and layouts at 390, 768 and 1366 pixels. Recording used generated microphone input. Physical student Chromebook microphones and school-device policies remain untested.
